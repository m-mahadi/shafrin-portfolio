The film emphasized the ethical responsibility of journalism, connected the story to several UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), and raised awareness about child rights, social inequality, and the need for responsible media reporting through creative visual communication.'
justification: 'This project explores the issue of child labor and the limited media attention given to important social problems in Bangladesh. Inspired by the realities of child workers in urban auto garages, the project examines how poverty, media censorship, and newsroom priorities can prevent these stories from being told. Through creative storytelling, the project aims to raise public awareness about child rights, media ethics, and social inequality while encouraging audiences to support responsible journalism and advocate for the protection and well-being of vulnerable children.'
