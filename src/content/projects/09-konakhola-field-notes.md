description: 'During the Summer 2025 semester I conducted a field research project for MSJ 3161. The study explored the livelihoods and environmental challenges faced by a marginalised Hindu community in Konakhola, Keraniganj. Using the Participatory Rural Appraisal approach, I applied Transect Walk, Farmers Field School and Resource Mapping to collect field data, documenting local agricultural practices, seasonal livelihoods, land use and resource distribution while recording how the community holds together under pressure.'
justification: 'I wanted to understand how local communities adapt to economic and environmental pressure using traditional knowledge and whatever resources are at hand, with particular attention to women''s contribution to household income. A participatory approach lets community members name their own needs, strengths and solutions rather than having them named for them.'
